Product Photo Resizer — Privacy Policy
Updated: 13 November 2026
Developer: Nobody Workshop
Contact: service@gamebox.wiki

Photos
Images you select are decoded, resized and saved on your device. We do not upload photos or file names to our servers. No account or server is required. The Android photo picker gives access only to the selected images; exports are saved through MediaStore. You can delete exports in your gallery.

Advertising
This version includes Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Banner ads appear on the home and saved-result screens. Editing and processing have no ads. Interstitials are disabled by default. Development builds use Google test ads. The Google advertising SDK may collect/share data such as IP address (which can infer general location), device/advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics and performance data for advertising, fraud prevention and measurement. It does not receive your selected photos from this app. Data practices also depend on consent, device settings and SDK configuration.
Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Choices
Where required, UMP presents a consent message before advertising requests. A required ad privacy choices entry appears in Settings, allowing you to revisit choices. If consent or advertising cannot be loaded, photo processing remains available. Android provides device-level advertising privacy controls.

Analytics and retention
Firebase Analytics is configured, with usage statistics off by default. When enabled in Settings, usage and ad lifecycle events may include app-instance identifiers and technical activity data, but never selected photos, filenames or file locations. You can disable collection in Settings. Crashlytics is not integrated. Firebase Remote Config downloads feature settings independently of the optional analytics switch; Firebase may process installation identifiers and technical request data for this service. Theme/language preferences, daily reward quota receipts and custom presets are stored locally. Google handles service data under its policies; we do not operate a separate analytics or advertising database.

Children and changes
This is a general photo tool, not designed for children. If the audience is changed to include children, advertising configuration and disclosures must be reviewed first. Policy updates will be provided in the app and at the public policy URL when available.

Optional rewarded videos are shown only when you request extra batch quota or skip an enabled wait. No extra quota is granted for an early exit before the earned-reward callback. Photo processing and the free 20-photo option remain available if advertising is unavailable. Standard interstitial ads are disabled by default in version 1.4.0.
