Product Photo Resizer — Privacy Policy Updated: 9 October 2026 Developer: Nobody Workshop Contact: service@gamebox.wiki Photos Images you select are decoded, resized and saved on your device. We do not upload photos or file names to our servers. No account or server is required. The Android photo picker gives access only to the selected images; exports are saved through MediaStore. You can delete exports in your gallery. Advertising This version includes Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Banner ads appear on the home and saved-result screens. Editing and processing have no ads. Interstitials are disabled by default. Development builds use Google test ads. The Google advertising SDK may collect/share data such as IP address (which can infer general location), device/advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics and performance data for advertising, fraud prevention and measurement. It does not receive your selected photos from this app. Data practices also depend on consent, device settings and SDK configuration. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy Google advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads Choices Where required, UMP presents a consent message before advertising requests. A required ad privacy choices entry appears in Settings, allowing you to revisit choices. If consent or advertising cannot be loaded, photo processing remains available. Android provides device-level advertising privacy controls. Analytics and retention No Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics project is configured. The app exposes a disabled, no-op analytics/revenue interface; it does not transmit custom analytics events. Photo or file information is excluded from that interface. Theme/language preferences are stored locally. Advertising data is processed and retained according to Google's policies; we do not operate a separate advertising database. Contact us for app questions and Google for data processed by its services. Children and changes This is a general photo tool, not designed for children. If the audience is changed to include children, advertising configuration and disclosures must be reviewed first. Policy updates will be provided in the app and at the public policy URL when available.
